Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Independent app. GrowthBridge is an independent learning app built for the Pi ecosystem. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the United Nations, any UN agency, any government, or Pi Network.

In short: your learning progress, actions, badges, name and certificates stay on your phone. We do not sell data, show ads, track you or use analytics.

1. Kept only on your phone

Your lesson progress, quiz results, daily actions, streaks, badges, the name you add for certificates, and your app settings are stored in your browser’s storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing the app’s data in the Pi Browser deletes them.

2. Pi sign-in

If you sign in with Pi, the official Pi SDK shares your Pi username and a temporary access token with the app on your phone. We do not store your username on a server and never see your passphrase or secret key.

3. Pi payments

If you choose to support the app, our server receives the payment ID from Pi Network and checks it with Pi Network to approve and complete it. Pi Network processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and the transaction is recorded on the public Pi blockchain. We do not keep a database of payments.

4. Certificates

Certificate images are created on your phone. They are only shared if you choose to share or download them.

5. Hosting

The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service.

6. What we never do

  • Sell or rent personal data.
  • Show advertising or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Ask for your Pi passphrase or secret key.

7. Children

The app is for general audiences. Pi Network accounts have their own age requirements.

8. Your choices

You can use the app without signing in, delete your data by clearing the app’s data in the Pi Browser, and contact us with any privacy question.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: growthbridge.app@gmail.com.
